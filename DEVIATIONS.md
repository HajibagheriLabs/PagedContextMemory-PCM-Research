| D-018 | 2026-11-14 | A2, synthetic tasks | Section 5.1/5.2 and prompt A2: S1 to S4 over a PG-19 haystack, target length within 1%, evidence depth stratified over 10 deciles, train generator with disjoint seeds, names and values | Choices where the specification is silent, all fixed in `configs/a2_tasks.yaml`: (1) the target length counts the **context** (the document, N of section 4.1), not the prompt; measured length lies in [0.99 x target, target].
